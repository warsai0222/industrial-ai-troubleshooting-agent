## Root Cause

(Correction to step 19 — same conclusion, now correctly citing the cause-vs-symptom check among its evidence.) Root cause: the 11:09-11:20 TE_8332A dip (bottoming at 517.05°C) is most consistent with an isolated instrumentation/signal fault on TE_8332A itself, not a genuine process upset. Every other candidate cause in the same guide (desuperheater overcooling, insufficient firing, load/firing mismatch, steam leak, waterside production loss) was checked against historian data and ruled out per step 17 — nothing else in the process moved. Confidence remains MEDIUM-HIGH (0.72), not HIGH, because this investigation could not directly check the DCS signal-quality flag or physically inspect the thermocouple.
